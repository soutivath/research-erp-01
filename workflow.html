Executive Answer

The workflow is correct for a real procurement and budget-control system if each transaction is blocked until the required setup, quota, budget, and approval checks are complete.

The main area to confirm with management is whether vendor comparison must happen before purchase order creation or during purchase order preparation.

End-To-End Operating Flow

This is the real business movement the development should follow. Each stage shows what the user does, what the system controls, and what must be true before the work can continue.

1

Foundation Setup

Build the operating base before anyone can request, approve, buy, or receive goods.

Workflow

1Company, department, position, and province setup
2User, role, permission, and department assignment
3Vendor, product, product unit, VAT, currency, bank, exchange rate
4Budget account, budget item, and budget detail setup
5Approval workflow, approval levels, and approver assignment
6Company product quantity quota setup
Control gate
  • Approvers must be defined.
  • Department must have a budget.
  • Product quota must exist before request items are submitted.
  • Document workflow must be approved before transactions use it.
2

Purchase Request

The requester asks for goods or services, linked to department, document, quota, and budget context.

Workflow

1Requester creates document and purchase request number
2Select product quota and unit
3Enter quantity, price, reason, and attachments
4System checks department budget exists
5System checks requested quantity against remaining quota
6System opens approval request and notifies approver
Control gate
  • Request date must be valid.
  • Quota cannot be exceeded.
  • Department budget must exist.
  • Request cannot move to purchase order until approved.
3

Purchase Request Approval

The business confirms the request is needed, appropriate, and allowed before procurement work continues.

Workflow

1Approver receives request notification
2Approver reviews requester, department, items, quantity, and reason
3Approver approves, rejects, or requests correction
4Approved request becomes available for purchase order
Decision point
  • If approved: continue to purchase order.
  • If rejected: stop the procurement flow.
  • If correction is needed: requester updates and resubmits.
4

Purchase Order

Procurement converts an approved request into a supplier-facing order with vendor, price, VAT, bank, and budget details.

Workflow

1Select approved purchase request
2Create purchase order document and purchase order number
3Copy approved request items into purchase order items
4Set price, VAT, selected vendor, and vendor bank account
5Attach supplier documents or quotation files when needed
6Assign budget item for spending control
Control gate
  • Purchase request must already be approved.
  • Selected vendor cannot be duplicated for the same item.
  • Vendor bank account must belong to the selected vendor.
  • Budget item must be assigned before receipt/payment control.
5

Purchase Order Approval

The business confirms the selected vendor, pricing, VAT, and spending line before receiving goods.

Workflow

1System opens purchase order approval
2Approver reviews selected vendor and order value
3Approver reviews budget item and supporting files
4Approved purchase order becomes available for receipt
Decision point
  • If approved: continue to receipt.
  • If rejected: procurement must correct or stop the order.
  • If budget assignment is missing: finance/procurement must complete it.
6

Receipt and Management Reporting

Receiving records the delivered goods, payment currency, exchange rate, and final amount for visibility and reporting.

Workflow

1Create receipt from approved purchase order
2Select received purchase order items
3System checks vendor bank account and exchange rate
4System calculates payment total in budget currency
5Receipt enters approval workflow
6Management reviews company summary, budget status, requests, orders, and receipts
Control gate
  • Purchase order must be approved before receipt.
  • Exchange rate must exist and be active.
  • Receipt should confirm actual received quantity.
  • Reports should show request, order, receipt, and budget status.

Who Does What

This view helps management understand ownership. The system is not only screens; it is a controlled handoff between departments.

Requester / Department

  • Creates purchase request.
  • Provides reason, quantity, item detail, and supporting file.
  • Corrects rejected or returned requests.
  • Confirms business need before procurement starts.

Approver / Management

  • Reviews purchase request.
  • Reviews purchase order value and vendor selection.
  • Reviews receipt where approval is required.
  • Uses reports to monitor budget and procurement movement.

Procurement / Finance

  • Maintains vendors, products, VAT, currency, bank, and exchange rate.
  • Creates purchase orders from approved requests.
  • Selects vendor and vendor bank account.
  • Assigns budget item for spending control.

System Control

  • Blocks unapproved workflow setup.
  • Blocks request when quota is exceeded.
  • Blocks purchase order when purchase request is not approved.
  • Blocks receipt when purchase order is not approved.

Real-World Fit Check

This is the part to discuss with the boss. It separates what is already correct from what policy decisions may still be needed.

Correct Flow

  • Setup before transaction work is correct.
  • Purchase request before purchase order is correct.
  • Purchase order before receipt is correct.
  • Approval gates before each major movement are correct.
  • Budget and quota checks before spending are correct.

Confirm With Boss

  • Should vendor comparison happen before purchase order or inside purchase order preparation?
  • Should receipt approval be required for every receipt or only above a value?
  • Should budget be reserved at purchase order approval or only counted when receipt is approved?
  • Should finance payment be a separate module after receipt?

Recommended Policy

  • Use purchase request approval to confirm business need.
  • Use purchase order approval to confirm vendor, price, and budget line.
  • Use receipt approval to confirm delivered goods and payment value.
  • Use reports to compare budget, ordered amount, received amount, and remaining balance.
